Publication Highlight

Quantitative Cerebral Perfusion Monitoring Using Transit-Time Flow Measurement During Neonatal Aortic Arch Surgery: A Case Report

Orioli V, Careddu L, Agulli M, Sperandeo F, Berardi M, Mangerini VF, Angeli E.

ASAIO Journal, June 9, 2026.

Neurological injury remains one of the most serious complications of neonatal cardiac surgery, particularly during procedures involving reconstruction of the aortic arch. Transit-time flow measurement (TTFM) allows real-time volumetric flow assessment and may represent an additional tool for intraoperative monitoring of cerebral perfusion.

These authors report the intraoperative use of TTFM for continuous monitoring of cerebral inflow during the repair of an interrupted aortic arch in a neonate. A flow probe was placed around the left common carotid artery to allow real-time measurement of carotid flow during cardiopulmonary bypass and selective cerebro-myocardial perfusion.

The flow measurements provided quantitative information complementary to conventional monitoring and were a useful adjunct for evaluating cerebral perfusion.

Congress Recap

ISMICS 2026: Innovation, Quality, and the Future of Robotic CABG

At this year's ISMICS Congress in Miami, a key takeaway was that completion control and quality assessment are just as critical in MIDCAB and robotic CABG procedures as they are in conventional open surgery.

Medistim hosted an Innovation Corner session with leading TCAB and MIDCAB surgeons, together with our External Innovation team, Håkon Grøthe and Rob Hurry. The discussions generated valuable ideas for future product improvements and reinforced the importance of close collaboration with customers to understand their evolving needs.

Another key observation was the growing momentum behind robotic cardiac surgery. While robotic CABG still represents only a small share of total procedures worldwide, the market is gaining traction, driven by INTUITIVE's renewed focus on cardiac surgery and the emergence of SS Innovations International.

The congress also marked a historic milestone for our Board member, Dr. Gry Dahle, who served as the first female President of ISMICS. As part of the program, she brought together great female leaders from cardiac surgery and the medical technology industry to share their career journeys and leadership experiences. Among them was our CEO, Kari E. Krogstad, who spoke about leadership, resilience, and growth.
